## Why

AI agents write code faster than people can read it. Teams face a choice: read everything and slow down, or skim and hope.

We build Slatis so a team can let agents work and still know what it merged, who checked it and who said yes.

## What we are building

**Proof with every change.**
Each change arrives with an evidence bundle, and the bundle opens with what nobody verified.

**Nobody approves their own work.**
The person who decides never built the change. On a team of one, the bundle says so.

**Only Slatis merges.**
No person or agent writes to the main line directly. A signed ledger that only grows records how each change got there.

**What can't be undone reaches a person.**
Deleting a database or emailing every customer always waits for a human.

## Where it stands

Live today: sign-in, organizations, and a signed ledger for every act. Next: your repositories on Slatis, imported from GitHub.
